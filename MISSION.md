# Learning mission

Understand enough about OCR deployment to choose, configure, and evaluate the independent OCR verifier for Simple Clean INT-02.

Success means we can:

- explain why the semantic model and OCR verifier are separate;
- run a local OCR service without sending warehouse photos to a third party;
- connect that service to the existing `INTAKE_RECOGNITION_*` configuration;
- compare PaddleOCR with managed and open-source alternatives; and
- promote a model only after the labeled evaluation shows no unsafe automatic acceptance.
